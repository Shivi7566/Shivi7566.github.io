Identity, end to end.

What I actually do, day to day, across enterprise identity platforms - and how I think about the work.

I'm an IAM Senior Engineer with 7.5+ years architecting and managing enterprise identity solutions across Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, AWS, and Oracle Cloud. I'm currently leading IAM operations across three client accounts at DXC Technology - two on Microsoft Entra ID, one on Okta - covering the full identity lifecycle: provisioning, governance, SSO/federation, conditional access, and hybrid identity synchronization.

My strength is in RBAC design, access governance, and compliance audits aligned to GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS. That means building identity systems that aren't just functional, but defensible - every access grant traceable, every review documented, every audit a formality rather than a fire drill.

I work closely with HR, IT, and Security teams to keep identity environments secure, scalable, and audit-ready - because identity sits at the intersection of all three, and a gap in any one of them becomes everyone's problem.
